01 / Tectonics

Honest Nigerian Materiality

Every design privileges locally sourced, climate-resilient assemblies—compressed laterite earth blocks (CEB), bush-hammered Abuja granite, board-formed concrete, and seasoned Nigerian Iroko timber that ages gracefully through Harmattan dust and rainy seasons.

02 / Savanna Climate

Solar Geometry & Courtyard Ventilation

Drawing from traditional Northern Nigerian courtyard compounds (Zaure thresholds and shaded verandas), deep roof cantilevers and perforated solar screens block high-angle afternoon heat while channeling cross-breezes naturally.
